01 — The Stunt

Skyhook.

A man in a suit tears onto an airfield on a motorcycle, throws a briefcase into a waiting car, and the car peels off. Then a plane comes in low, hooks him out of the moving car, and he cuts away at altitude and parachutes to the ground.

Shot like a Bond cold open. Motorcycle to car to plane to skydive.

01
The escape

Suit and helmet, tearing onto an airfield on a motorcycle like he’s running from something.

02
The drop

Hard stop. He’s off the bike and running before it’s settled.

03
The car

A 1win convertible is waiting, people inside waving him over. Briefcase in, he jumps in, they peel off.

04
The hook

He rigs up at speed. The plane passes overhead trailing a rope. He catches it and clips in.

05
The extraction

The line goes tight and pulls him out of the car into the sky. The car keeps driving without him.

06
The skydive

He cuts away at altitude, freefalls, and flies his canopy down.

03 — Deliverables

04 — Budget

Skyhook
$30,000

One flat fee. Everything is inside it.

  • Aircraft, pilots and the extraction rigging
  • Stunt coordinator and safety crew
  • Three full days of training before we shoot
  • Full film crew and production on the day
  • Car rental and 1win branding on the car
  • Permits, insurance and vehicles
  • All editing, through to six finished reels
  • Every frame of raw footage handed to 1win

The original $22,500 covered the extraction on its own. The difference is the production around it — the crew, the training days, the vehicles and the branding that turn a stunt into a film.
